Once upon a time, there was an ugly duckling. He was very fuzzy and gray, and looked nothing like the other ducklings. Because of how he looked, the poor, ugly, fuzzy duckling often thought he was a mistake. It didn’t help that the other ducklings made fun of the ugly duckling, calling him dumb, gross, and of course, ugly. 

Given all of this it is no wonder that the ugly duckling spent most of his time by himself, eating bananas, and drawing in his sketchbook with his favorite pencil. He rarely made mistakes when he drew, for though he was ugly, this fuzzy gray duckling was an inspired artist. 

After many years and much practice the ugly duckling grew into an ugly duck, and learned how to ignore the mean comments and surround himself with other fowl that loved him for who he was. In time, he began to believe himself as beautiful as his pencil drawings (which were world famous by now). And with that belief, it became the truth, for perception is reality.
